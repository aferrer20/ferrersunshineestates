Privacy Policy

St. Petersburg, Florida

Ferrer Sunshine Estates (operated by Baez Perez Estate Management LLC, "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit ferrersunshineestates.com or inquire about a stay, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Last updated September 8, 2026
01

Information we collect

  • Inquiry & contact details — name, email, phone number, and message content submitted through our contact form or direct inquiry form.
  • Booking & lease details — occupant names, dates of stay, and related information submitted through our lease registration process, used solely to prepare and administer your reservation.
  • Payment information — payments are processed by our banking partner (Chase) and third-party card processors. We do not store full card numbers on our servers.
  • Usage data — standard technical information (browser type, pages viewed, approximate location, referring site) collected automatically to help us understand site traffic and improve the experience.
02

How we use your information

  • To respond to inquiries and confirm availability
  • To prepare lease registrations and manage your reservation
  • To process payments and refundable security deposits
  • To send stay-related communications (check-in instructions, invoices, receipts)
  • To maintain the security and performance of this website

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

03

Third-party services

We use a small number of trusted vendors to operate this site and our booking process, including form-processing (Formspree), transactional email delivery, and website hosting (Netlify). Each of these providers processes data only as needed to perform their service and maintains their own privacy and security practices.

04

Cookies

This site may use minimal cookies or local storage required for core functionality (such as remembering your place in a form). We do not use third-party advertising trackers.

05

Data retention & security

We retain inquiry and lease records for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill your stay, meet legal and accounting obligations, and resolve any disputes, after which records are securely deleted or archived. We use industry-standard safeguards to protect information in transit and at rest, though no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

06

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting us using the details below. Florida residents and guests may have additional rights under applicable state law; we will honor valid requests to the extent required.

07

Contact us
