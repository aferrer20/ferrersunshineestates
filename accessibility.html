Accessibility Statement

St. Petersburg, Florida

Ferrer Sunshine Estates is committed to making ferrersunshineestates.com usable by as many people as possible, including guests who use assistive technology.

01

Our approach

We aim to align this site with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, where practical — including descriptive image alternative text, readable color contrast, keyboard-navigable menus and forms, and clear heading structure. Accessibility is an ongoing effort, and we review and improve the site on a rolling basis.

02

Booking assistance

If any part of this website or our online booking process is difficult to use with your assistive technology, we're glad to complete your inquiry or lease registration by phone or email instead.

03

Feedback

If you encounter an accessibility barrier on this site, please let us know — including the page and a brief description of the issue — and we'll work to address it. Contact Amanda@bpestatemgmt.com or (727) 504-9949.
